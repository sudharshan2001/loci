# Loci

**Loci gives AI glasses and robots a private memory of the places you move through.**

Named after the *method of loci*, the memory-palace technique of tying memories to places.
Walk through a space with a camera at eye level. Loci finds the objects, reads shop signs, notes the
state of things like a lit stove, places everything in 3D with metric scale, and keeps it on your machine.
Then ask "where did I leave my keys?" and get an answer with a 3D location, a redacted proof frame and a
robot-ready goal.

> Work in progress. See `PROGRESS.md`.
